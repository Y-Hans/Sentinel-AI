# HARD-NEGATIVE FORENSIC ANALYSIS

## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents a detailed forensic analysis of the false positives generated by the current champion (Logistic Regression / Rule Hybrid) on the curated hard-negative dataset. The model consistently misclassifies legitimate institutional and government warnings as malicious phishing, despite their benign nature.

## 2. Analyzed False Positives

We identified three distinct semantic families that trigger false positives:

### Family A: TAX_NOTICE
- **Example Text**: `Income Tax Department: Non-filing of ITR for AY <NUM_CODE>-<NUM> may attract late fee under Section 234F. File return on official portal incometax.gov.in.`
- **Sender**: AD-INCOMET
- **Source**: SRC_CURATED_HARD_NEGATIVES_V1
- **Model Confidence (Malicious)**: 1.0000
- **Extracted Signals**: Threat of penalty (`late fee`), financial vocabulary, and a URL (`incometax.gov.in`).
- **Primary Failure Reason**: SEMANTIC AMBIGUITY. The lexical overlap between a real tax penalty notice and a fake tax penalty phishing scam is nearly 100%. The model lacks the capacity to distinguish legitimate sender structures (e.g. `AD-INCOMET`) and official domain structures (`.gov.in`) from generic phishing templates because the penalty/URL signals overwhelm the linear weights.

### Family B: ELECTRICITY_WARNING
- **Example Text**: `MSEDCL: Consumer No <NUM_CODE>, bill amount <AMOUNT> is due on <DATE>. Pay promptly through Mahavitaran official app.`
- **Sender**: AX-MAHADIS
- **Source**: SRC_CURATED_HARD_NEGATIVES_V1
- **Model Confidence (Malicious)**: 1.0000
- **Extracted Signals**: Urgency (`promptly`, `due`), Financial (`bill amount`), CTA (`app`).
- **Primary Failure Reason**: DATA & FEATURE. The model is severely penalized by the presence of electricity bill vocabulary combined with urgency. We do not have sufficient contrastive examples of legitimate bill reminders versus electricity disconnection scams (e.g., "power will be cut tonight").

### Family C: KYC_UPDATE / UNAUTHORIZED_LOGIN
- **Example Text**: `UIDAI Notification: Your Aadhaar authentication was performed on <DATE> via Biometric at Telecom Partner. If unauthorized, lock Aadhaar at resident.uidai.gov.in.`
- **Sender**: AD-UIDAIG
- **Source**: SRC_CURATED_HARD_NEGATIVES_V1
- **Model Confidence (Malicious)**: 0.9560
- **Extracted Signals**: KYC (`Aadhaar`), Fear/Threat (`unauthorized`), URL (`uidai.gov.in`).
- **Primary Failure Reason**: SEMANTIC AMBIGUITY. Protective warnings ("If unauthorized") use the exact same vocabulary as fear-inducing phishing ("Unauthorized login detected, verify KYC"). The bag-of-words and deterministic rules treat the word "unauthorized" as a threat signal rather than a protective conditional clause.

## 3. Root Cause Assessment

- **Linear Model Capacity**: Logistic Regression simply adds weights. A high positive weight on "unauthorized" or "penalty" will trigger a malicious classification regardless of the context (e.g., "If unauthorized...").
- **Semantic Ambiguity**: The same observable evidence maps to conflicting labels based on nuanced context.
- **Data Gap**: Lack of paired contrastive training examples (Legit Warning vs Fake Warning) to teach the model the difference.

## 4. Next Steps (Phase C & D)
We will proceed to architecture search and semantic contrastive training. We must investigate models that can learn feature interactions and contextual embeddings, rather than relying on linear additive weights.
